About me

Hi, I'm Hannah — a Product/UX Designer who's curious by nature, always most at home in spaces where the design problems are still being figured out.

Currently at Jet Charge as Product Designer, focused on EV charging experiences across residential and commercial markets. Before that, two years at Evergen deep in distributed energy resources — building app experiences for Zelora (Bunnings-backed solar & battery), Enreal (home electrification), and Pooled by Poolwerx (smart pool energy management).

Across these roles I've designed for a wide range of users — homeowners managing solar and batteries, commercial operators, EV drivers, pool owners — all within systems where real-time data, hardware behaviour, and energy costs intersect. It's a complex and important space, and one I've come to know well.

Previously at Creativemass on WealthConnect, a sophisticated financial planning platform built on Salesforce. I worked across multiple product squads solving complex problems for financial advisors.

Before that, UX/UI Designer in the Innovation Team at Resolution Digital, building new experiences for retainer clients.

And before my UX career — I worked as a product developer in the garment industry for brands like Article One, M.J. Bale, and Bond-eye Australia. Design has always been part of who I am.
